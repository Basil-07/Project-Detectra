About Detectra

Detectra is a research and educational application for exploring infrared spectra, characteristic peaks, and machine-learning-assisted compound identification.

The platform combines XGBoost classifiers, classifier-chain ensembles, spectrum visualization, and PDF reporting. Results require independent scientific validation before use in clinical, legal, or forensic decisions.
